The problem

Campaigns lose elections in the gaps between tools.

Facebook lives in one tab. Volunteers in a spreadsheet. Agents on WhatsApp. Donations in Paystack. Election night on paper. Nobody sees the same picture.

  • Blind ground game — no live map of who is at which PU.
  • Untrained volunteers — codes and courses scattered.
  • No election desk — results arrive too late to act.

One command center for the whole campaign.

CCC gives your directors, media team, field agents, and finance one shared source of truth — with permissions so each role only sees what they should.
